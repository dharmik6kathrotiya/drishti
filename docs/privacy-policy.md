# Drishti Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

Drishti is a free, open-source astrology app. It is designed so that **your personal data stays on your phone**.

## What we collect

**Nothing on our servers.** Drishti has no accounts, no login and no backend. We do not receive, store or sell your:

- names or birth details (date, time, place),
- charts, readings, questions to the AI helper or its memory,
- journal entries, moods or tarot readings,
- palm photos.

All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app or using **Settings → Delete all data** removes it.

## Sharing you control

- **Profile sharing:** a QR code or text code you choose to show or send contains only the name and birth details of that profile. It goes directly from phone to phone.
- **Export:** "Export my data" creates a text backup that you choose where to send.

## Network use

Drishti works offline. It connects to the internet only for:

1. **Ads** (Google AdMob). Ads are the app's only income. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data as described in [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Where required by law we ask for your consent first (Google User Messaging Platform). Users aged 13–17 receive only non-personalised ads with a "G" content rating.
2. **The optional daily feed:** a public, static file of general content (announcements, general sign horoscopes). The request contains no personal data.
3. **The optional AI model download,** only when you start it. You can restrict it to Wi-Fi.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why | When |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | Scan a profile QR code; optional palm photo for the palmistry guide | Only when you open those screens |
| Photos | Choose a palm photo | Only when you choose one |
| Notifications | Reminders you turn on (all are off by default) | Only when you turn one on |
| Internet | Ads, optional feed, optional model download | See above |

## Children

Drishti is for ages 13 and above. Users aged 13–17 get a teen mode: no marriage matching, no adult topics, and non-personalised ads only. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, because personal data never leaves the device.

## Your rights (India DPDP Act 2023, GDPR and others)

Because we hold no personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on your behalf. You can view, export and delete all of your data in the app at any time. Questions about ads data should be directed to Google.

## Changes

Changes to this policy will be published in the app's open-source repository, and the date above will be updated.
