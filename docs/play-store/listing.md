## App content forms

**Ads:** Yes, the app contains ads (Google AdMob).

**Advertising ID:** Yes, used for ads (the AdMob SDK adds the AD_ID permission).

**Target audience:** 13–15, 16–17 and 18+. Not designed for children under 13. Users under 18 get teen mode: no matching or adult topics, and only non-personalised ads rated "G".

**Content rating questionnaire (IARC):** choose the category "All other app types". Answer No to violence, sexual content, profanity, drugs, gambling and fear. Answer No to user interaction and to sharing location; profiles are shared only phone to phone by QR code. Answer No to digital purchases.

**Data safety.** The app itself collects nothing; everything stays on the phone. Declare only what the AdMob SDK collects:

| Data type | Collected | Shared | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Device or other IDs (advertising ID) | Yes | Yes (Google) | Advertising, fraud prevention |
| Approximate location (from IP address) | Yes | Yes (Google) | Advertising, fraud prevention |
| App interactions | Yes | Yes (Google) | Advertising, analytics |
| Crash logs and diagnostics | Yes | Yes (Google) | Analytics, fraud prevention |

- Data is encrypted in transit: Yes.
- Users can request deletion: the app holds no data on any server. Personal data is deleted in the app under Settings → Delete all data.
- Data collection is required (ads cannot be turned off), and it is processed by Google.

**Health:** the app is not a medical app. The children reading and the Nadi dosha advice say clearly that they are traditional readings, not medical assessments.

**Government app / financial features / news:** No.
